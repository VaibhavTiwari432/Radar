WHAT EACH OUTCOME MEANS, WRITTEN DOWN FIRST
-------------------------------------------

  A_coverage >= 85%   The amplitude score TRACKED the NumTraining-driven slope
                      shift. The exchangeability limit is real in mechanism
                      but does not bind on this observer grid.

  A_coverage <  85%   The score was BLIND to the shift. The label distribution
                      moved while the score distribution did not -- the
                      violation the guarantee is conditional on. The limit
                      binds, and no coverage number may be quoted for a radar
                      whose CFAR training length is unknown without pooling.

Neither outcome is a "pass" and neither is a "fail". One says a documented
limit is weaker than feared; the other says it is load-bearing. Both are
results.


AMENDMENT POLICY
----------------

None. The rule is not edited in light of the data.

If the results say something the rule cannot express, that goes in a NEW
section of ASSURANCE_LAYER_RESULTS.md as a limitation OF THE RULE, with the
rule's own verdict reported first and unaltered. Precedent in this repo:
experiments.cliffRootCause pre-registered a hypothesis, the data refuted it,
and the refutation was published as the finding rather than the hypothesis
being quietly rewritten.
